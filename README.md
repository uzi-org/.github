# Origins

Uzi is the Swahili name for "thread" or "string." We came up with this name because of our vision to connect the world to Africa. Africa has so much rich history, many untold stories, unique culture, and raw experiences that the world needs to know, hear, and immerse themselves in. Lengo letu ni kushona mambo haya yote jinsi unavyotumia uzi kushona nguo. I will call the organization Uzi Org.

Translation of the Swahili sentence:
Lengo letu ni kushona mambo haya yote jinsi unavyotumia uzi kushona nguo.
— Our goal is to stitch all these things together just as you use a thread to sew clothes.
